01 — The Denial Crisis

Hospitals deliver the care.
Then they fight to get paid for it.

Claim denials are not edge cases. They are a structural feature of American healthcare finance — a multi-billion-dollar friction layer sitting between treatment and payment.

Providers lose billions annually to denials they never appeal, and the patterns behind those denials — which payers, which codes, which facilities, which denial reasons — are known in fragments, never in full.

MedicalDenials.com names the discipline that assembles the full picture. It is the natural headquarters for the denial-prevention platform, the appeal-intelligence dataset, or the RCM product line that turns denials from cost center into competitive edge.
